Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request deletion of personal information processed by Velora.

How to Request Deletion

Contact the Velora application administrator and provide sufficient information to identify the relevant account or phone number.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on applicable requirements, deletion may include phone numbers, notification metadata, message references, and related user information.

Processing Time

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to legal, security, and operational retention requirements.

Contact

For a data deletion request, contact the Velora application administrator.
